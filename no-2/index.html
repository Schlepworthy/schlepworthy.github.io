Where I went last weekend · No schlep · free

Long Bridge Park, for the planes

Low-key weekend, so this one's close to home: a Saturday walk at Long Bridge Park. It's free, there's a view of the monuments, and you can sit and watch the planes take off from the airport. My wife and I sat and chilled for a while, then got our cardio in walking the park. Swing by Tatte for a pistachio latte, or Colada Shop for an empanada and a Cuban-style coffee, to carry while you stroll. The signs along the path have cute info about the birds there, so birders should bring binoculars. You might get lucky.

475 Long Bridge Drive · open sunrise to 11 p.m. · free · checked live Nov. 9 on Arlington County's park page

The holiday market opens

Editor's
pick

The DowntownDC Holiday Market is back for its 22nd year, and Friday is day one. Two blocks of F Street in front of the National Portrait Gallery fill with 121 vendors: prints, candles, tea, hot sauce, and food stalls that run from raclette to churros. It's free to walk, dogs are welcome, and it's five Yellow Line stops from Crystal City. Go after work, see it lit up, then get a bowl of ramen a six-minute walk away.

  1. 5:30 p.m.Yellow Line to Gallery PlaceFive stops from Crystal City. Take the 7th and F exit and you come up at the market.
  2. 5:50 p.m.Duck into the Portrait GalleryFree, no passes needed, open until 7 p.m. The entrance is at Eighth and G streets, a block north of the market.
  3. 6:30 p.m.Walk the marketF Street between Seventh and Ninth, open until 9. The 15 food and drink stalls include Stable DC's raclette, The Frenchman's Crepes and Don Churro.
  4. 7:45 p.m.Ramen at Daikaya705 Sixth St. NW. Walk-ins only, open until 10:30 on Fridays. Bowls run $17.50-$21.
  5. 9 p.m.Yellow Line homeBack to Gallery Place, five stops to Crystal City.

Before you go

  • Free that morning?The ribbon-cutting is 10-11 a.m., with remarks and live entertainment. The stalls open to everyone at 11.
  • No stage acts Friday nightThe main stage's live sets start Thursday, Nov. 19, then run Thursday through Sunday. Friday is the stalls, the lights and holiday music.
  • It's all outdoorsFriday is partly sunny and windy, with gusts to 25 mph and a high near 51, so bring a warm layer.

Under the Lincoln

Editor's
pick

The National Park Service opened the undercroft beneath the Lincoln Memorial in June: about 15,000 square feet of exhibits in a space the public had never seen, on how the memorial was built and how its meaning has changed. Walk in from Arlington over Memorial Bridge, then ride home for Freddie's $16.99 buffet brunch.

  1. 10 a.m.Blue Line to Arlington CemeteryThree stops from Crystal City.
  2. 10:15 a.m.Over Memorial BridgeAbout 20 minutes on foot (0.9 mile), river on both sides and the Lincoln straight ahead.
  3. 10:45 a.m.Your undercroft slotThe line starts at the entrance doors on the north side of the Lincoln Memorial steps. Get there five minutes early, and allow about an hour inside.
  4. NoonBack over the bridgeBlue Line home from Arlington Cemetery.
  5. 12:45 p.m.Freddie's buffet brunch555 23rd St. S. All you can eat, 11 a.m.-3 p.m., $16.99 with a mimosa, coffee or soft drink, plus dessert.

Before you go

  • TicketsFree, with a $1 nonrefundable fee per ticket to book on Recreation.gov. Everyone 2 and up needs one.
  • Fri., 4 p.m.More Saturday tickets drop then. On Nov. 9 the 10:45 slot had 40 left; the 10:15 had 10.
  • Free same dayThe kiosk at the Korean War Veterans Memorial hands out same-day tickets from 8:45 a.m., first come. One person over 16 can ask for up to six.
  • Leave at homeKnives, multitools and strollers. Late arrivals can be turned away.

Eagles on Mason Neck

Editor's
pick

Mason Neck is a peninsula on the Potomac in Lorton, where a state park and a national wildlife refuge sit side by side and bald eagles are around all year. Start at Gunston Hall, George Mason's home, walk the boardwalk trail out over the Great Marsh, then catch the state park's Eagle Watch with binoculars on loan.

  1. 9:15 a.m.Leave Crystal CityI-395 and I-95 south to Exit 163, Route 1, then Gunston Road. About 30 minutes, 22 miles.
  2. 10 a.m.Gunston Hall10709 Gunston Road. Guided house tours on the hour (10, 11, then 1-4), and grounds that run down to the river: a restored garden, a viewing mount over the Potomac, trails. Adults $12 online, fees included.
  3. NoonThe Great Marsh TrailThree-quarters of a mile, paved and accessible, ending at a platform over the 250-acre Great Marsh. The refuge calls it its best spot for eagles. Trailhead on Gunston Road, a mile east of High Point Road. Pack lunch.
  4. 1 p.m.Woodmarsh Trail, if you're up for itA 3-mile loop off High Point Road with a covered platform and telescope about a mile in. Muddy after rain.
  5. 2:30 p.m.Eagle WatchMason Neck State Park visitor center, 7301 High Point Road. An interpreter talks eagles and Mason Neck history, and you can borrow binoculars to scan Belmont Bay. Free with the $10 parking; no registration.
  6. 3:30 p.m.Bay View TrailAbout a mile on the trail named for its bay views, while the light lasts. The park closes at dusk.
  7. 5 p.m.Dinner in OccoquanAbout 20 minutes away. Madigan's Waterfront on Mill Street, or the Secret Garden Cafe farther up Mill. Parking in town is free.
  8. About 7 p.m.HomeRoute 123 to I-95 north, about 30 minutes, more if I-95 is backed up.

Why Sunday

  • Hunt daysThe park and the refuge hold managed deer hunts on Tuesdays, Nov. 17, Dec. 1 and Dec. 8. Not this weekend.
  • SaturdayNov. 14 is the hunters' scouting day, 9 a.m.-4 p.m., and neither agency says what that means for visitors. Sunday has nothing on the hunt calendar, and it's the Eagle Watch day.
  • ClosedThe Marsh View Trail and its lot, for construction. Boat rentals are done for the season.
  • Refuge hours7 a.m.-5 p.m. through March. Sunset is 4:54.

  1. 5:15 p.m.Yellow Line to Eisenhower AvenueFive stops from Crystal City.
  2. 5:30 p.m.Walk to Cameron Run4001 Eisenhower Ave. It's a real walk from the station: about 1.6 miles west along Eisenhower Avenue, about 40 minutes, mapped Nov. 8. Or drive; NOVA Parks doesn't post parking details.
  3. DarkLights, maze, mini golfIt's fully dark by about 5:25.
  4. 8 p.m.Yellow Line homeFive stops back.
Tickets
$9 online, $12 at the gate, plus tax and fees: 2 × $9 = $18 online. No refunds, but you can rebook before your date.
What's in it
The park lists the lights, the maze, a scavenger hunt, mini golf and "a hot beverage and snack" without saying which cost extra.
Later
Weekends only through Nov. 22, Friday through Sunday from Nov. 27 through Dec. 17, then nightly from Dec. 18 through Jan. 3.
Checked
