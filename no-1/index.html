Diwali after dark on the Mall

Schlepworthy

The National Museum of Asian Art stays open late for the festival of lights: food vendors on the plaza, a sarod recital, dance sets, crafts, and a DJ once the sun is down. Free, 5-9 p.m. with the DJ until 9:30, three Yellow Line stops from Crystal City.

  1. 5:15 p.m.Yellow Line to L'Enfant PlazaThree stops from Crystal City, then about a 10-minute walk west along Independence Avenue to 1050 Independence Ave. SW.
  2. 5:30-7 p.m.Plaza and galleriesDance sets on the West Building plaza at 5:30, 6:15 and 7. Food from Indian diaspora vendors, sold on the plaza. Family crafts until 7; diya coloring and rangoli run to 9, and docent talks go on through the evening.
  3. 7:15 p.m.Sarod recitalMeyer Auditorium, the second of two sets (the first is at 6). Limited seats, first come.
  4. 7:30-9:30 p.m.After-hours dance partyDJ Maiya on the plaza, then three stops home.
Cost
Free. Budget for vendor food and Metro fare. Registration on Eventbrite is recommended, not required, and doesn't guarantee entry; most activities are first come, first served.
Where
National Museum of Asian Art, 1050 Independence Ave. SW. The Blue Line to Smithsonian is a shorter walk but a longer ride.
Weather
Showers are likely Friday morning, mainly before 8 a.m., then it turns mostly cloudy. By the 5 p.m. start the rain chance is about 10%, and it falls to near zero by 7, per the National Weather Service on Nov. 5. The sun sets at 5:02 and it drops to about 61 degrees by 7 p.m. The food vendors and dance sets are on the outdoor plaza, so bring a jacket.
Checked
Live on Nov. 1, 3 and 5: the museum's own Eventbrite page (date, 5-9 p.m., full program) and the washington.org listing. Eventbrite showed few registrations left Thursday morning.
The fine print
  • The museum's own events page doesn't list the program, so it comes from the museum's Eventbrite page. It gets rechecked Thursday.
  • Eventbrite's header says 5-9 p.m., but the museum's own calendar runs the night to 9:30, and the DJ set goes until 9:30.
  • Friday's Blue Line single-tracking is 9 a.m.-3 p.m. south of Van Dorn Street, so it doesn't touch this trip.

Short schlep · free · Metro

A free Saturday in Old Town

Schlepworthy

A farmers market the city has run for more than 260 years (this year across the street from Market Square, while City Hall is renovated) in the morning, crafts and story time on the Carlyle House lawn at midday, and Chopin and Rachmaninoff at the Lyceum to close it out.

  1. MorningOld Town Farmers' MarketTemporarily on the 100 block of N. Royal St. and the Tavern Square courtyard, across from Market Square, 7 a.m.-noon, year-round, rain or shine. Blue or Yellow to King St-Old Town, then about a mile down King Street, or the free DASH 30 or 31 bus to Fairfax and King. The free King Street Trolley runs from 11 a.m.
  2. Noon-3 p.m.Carlyle House Family Fun Day121 N. Fairfax St. Story time and crafts on the front lawn, for all ages. Free, with donations encouraged.
  3. 4 p.m."An Autumn in Old Town" piano concertThe Lyceum, 201 S. Washington St. Doors at 3:45. About an hour of Chopin and Rachmaninoff with a short intermission. Free, with an Eventbrite RSVP. Eventbrite still showed few spots left Thursday morning, Nov. 5, so RSVP early.
Cost
Free, plus Metro fare and whatever you buy at the market.
Parking
If you drive, the Tavern Square and Courthouse garages are free during market hours; the Market Square Garage is closed for the renovation. No pets at the market.
Checked
Live on Nov. 3 and 5: the city's farmers market page, NOVA Parks' Carlyle House event page and the concert's Eventbrite page.

Sunday

One train ride to a market, one short ride to a show.

Short schlep, twice

A market by day, a ukulele legend by night

Schlepworthy

Start at the Four Mile Run Farmers and Artisans Market in Arlandria, then come back to the same stretch of Mount Vernon Avenue that night for Jake Shimabukuro at the Birchmere, the area's classic listening room.

  1. 9 a.m.-1 p.m.Four Mile Run Farmers and Artisans Market4109 Mount Vernon Ave., Alexandria. Farmers, local makers and prepared food, with live music on the market stage from about 10 a.m. to noon. No Metro stop nearby, so it's a short drive or bike ride.
  2. 7:30 p.m.Jake Shimabukuro at the Birchmere3701 Mount Vernon Ave., Alexandria. The ukulele player who blends jazz, rock, blues and Hawaiian music. Tickets are $49.50 before fees: $56.50 at the box office, $63.45 through Ticketmaster. Doors at 5, and seating at the tables is first come, so go early. Potomac Yard is the closest Metro, about a 25-minute walk, so a short drive or rideshare is easier.
Cost
For two: (2 × $0 market) + (2 × $56.50 box office tickets) = $113.00, before food, drinks and tax. Through Ticketmaster: 2 × $63.45 = $126.90.
Prefer French?
Bar Colline, 269 19th Court S., is open 11 a.m.-9 p.m. Sunday, with happy hour at the bar 4-7 p.m.
Checked
Live on Nov. 3 and 5: the Birchmere's own event page, and the market's own site and Instagram, which say Sundays 9 a.m.-1 p.m. through Nov. 22. The city's market page lists Four Mile Run as closed for the season; the market's own Oct. 25 post wins.

Rather ride?

Take the Mount Vernon Trail south toward Old Town for fall color. Up at Gravelly Point, the trail detours closer to the GW Parkway around the Long Bridge work, a bypass the Mount Vernon Gazette reported in September will stay for the next few years.

    Schubert's "Shepherd on a Rock," Mendelssohn's D minor trio and Brahms' G minor piano quartet, opening the group's 20th season. Free parking, a short drive.

  • Astronomy for Everyone at Sky Meadows

    5-8 p.m. · Sky Meadows State Park, Delaplane · $10 parking per car

    A NASA JPL talk, then telescopes with the Northern Virginia Astronomy Club in an International Dark-Sky Park. Entrance gates close at 7. Car only, a big schlep, so bring chairs or a blanket and make a day of it: First Saturdays runs 11 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday night looks mostly clear with a low near 46 at Reagan National, and it runs colder out there, so dress warm.

  • Ginkgo Gold Weekend at Blandy Experimental Farm

    10 a.m.-4 p.m. Sat. and Sun. · State Arboretum of Virginia, Boyce · Free

    A reader tip from r/nova: the arboretum's ginkgo grove should be near peak gold this weekend, so get there before noon for the best light. Food trucks Saturday, and the picnic area by the parking is pretty too. Park by the Quarters building and walk about 10 minutes to the grove. Car only, a big schlep, and it pairs well with the Sky Meadows astronomy night.
