Where I went last weekend · Real schlep · about $17

Xi'an Famous Foods, finally

A reader on Reddit told me to try Xi'an Famous Foods in Clarendon, and last Friday night I finally went. Of course I got the hand-ripped noodles with the spicy and tingly beef. It was insanely good. I got a bottled orange soda with it, which was really good too, and the whole thing came to about $17. I haven't tried anything else yet, but I'm already looking forward to going back for the dumplings. The place is clean, and it's tucked into a cool little alcove in a nice neighborhood, so I like the trip over there even from Crystal City, with all the shops nearby. I also tried to talk my wife into a bar pie at Colony Grill, in the same complex, because a good friend recommends theirs. One tip: black vinegar on everything. I saw a guy put it on his noodles and thought, sure, on dumplings, but noodles? It was delicious, and it added a nice touch.

2800 Clarendon Blvd., Suite 1500 Chain · open every day, 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. · on the menu it's the Spicy & Tingly Beef Muscle Hand-Ripped Noodles · checked live Nov. 17 on xianfoods.com

Getting there: Blue Line to Rosslyn, then the Orange or Silver Line two stops to Clarendon. Blue and Orange trains are 24 minutes apart this weekend. Colony Grill Chain is also at 2800 Clarendon Blvd.; checked live Nov. 17 on its site.

Union Market lights its tree

Editor's
pick

Union Market kicks off its holiday season Friday at 6 with a live holiday performance and seasonal drink specials. It's free. It's also opening night for the Dome on the roof: a heated, 50-foot dome with a 360-degree projection show, done up this winter as a 1970s Alpine hotel. The Dome takes tickets. The tree doesn't.

  1. 5:15 p.m.Yellow Line to Gallery PlaceFive stops from Crystal City. Change to the Red Line toward Glenmont.
  2. 5:35 p.m.Red Line to NoMa-Gallaudet UThree stops. From the station it's a right on Florida Avenue NE, then a left on Fourth or Fifth Street, per Union Market's directions. It's about a 10-minute walk.
  3. 6 p.m.The tree lightingAt the market, 1309 Fifth St. NE. It runs until 8.
  4. 6:45 p.m.Dinner in the hallThe District Fishwife's kitchen runs until 8. Fossette Focacceria's sandwiches run $15-$18. Across Fourth Street at La Cosecha, LC Pupusas sells two pupusas and a drink for $12, until 10.
  5. 8:15 p.m.Up to the DomeIf you booked. Friday's shows are at 6:30, 8:15 and 10 p.m., all 21 and over, $36 a person. On Thursday morning the 8:15 was down to tables for four; pairs could still book the 6:30 or the 10. Check in 15 to 30 minutes early. The elevator is at the market's front-east entrance, Sixth Street and Neal Place.
  6. 9:45 p.m.Red Line homeNoMa-Gallaudet U back to Gallery Place, then the Yellow Line. Metro runs until 2 a.m. Friday.

Before you go

The bagel run

Editor's
pick

I handed down a bagel ruling this week (it's in Eat), so here's a morning built around the winner. Brooklyn Bagel Bakery is in Courthouse. The farmers market is a short walk from it and runs until noon. Then it's about a mile downhill on Wilson Boulevard to Rosslyn, where the holiday market has live music until 12:30 and Bethesda Bagels is around the corner if you want a second opinion.

  1. 8:45 a.m.Blue Line to RosslynFour stops from Crystal City. Blue Line trains are 24 minutes apart this weekend, so check the next-train board before you leave the house.

  5. 10:45 a.m.Walk down Wilson BoulevardAbout a mile, about 20 minutes, all downhill.
  6. 11:10 a.m.Rosslyn's holiday marketCentral Place Plaza, 1800 N. Lynn St., at the Rosslyn Metro. Local makers, live music by Jessica Allossery until 12:30, and free bow making until 1.

Before you go

  • The lineIt can be long at Brooklyn Bagel, and it moves fast. They take cards with a small minimum, about $10. Cash might save you a minute.

Big schlep · car only · $30 a car

Last call at Big Meadows

Shenandoah National Park never closes, but Big Meadows does. The visitor center, the lodge and its dining room shut for the season Nov. 29, and Saturday is the dry day. Hike down to a waterfall, eat lunch in the lodge, and get off the mountain before dark. Figure it's about 10 degrees cooler up there.

  1. 7:30 a.m.Leave Crystal CityI-66 west to Exit 43A, U.S. 29 south to Warrenton, then U.S. 211 west to the Thornton Gap entrance, per the Park Service's directions. It's about an hour and 40 minutes before traffic.
  2. 9:30 a.m.Thornton Gap entranceMile 31.5 of Skyline Drive. It's $30 a car, good for seven days. Card or phone only, no cash.
  3. 10:15 a.m.Byrd Visitor CenterMile 51, open 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. It's 19.5 miles south of the gate, and the speed limit is 35.
  4. 10:45 a.m.Dark Hollow FallsThe trailhead is at mile 50.7. It's 1.4 miles round trip with a 440-foot climb back out, and the Park Service allows an hour and 15 minutes. It's steep, and slippery when wet. No pets on this one.
  5. 12:30 p.m.Lunch at Big Meadows LodgeThe Spottswood Dining Room serves lunch from noon to 2:30. A turkey melt is $17.49, and the blackberry ice cream pie is $12.99.
  6. 2 p.m.One more viewHawksbill Summit, at mile 45.5 on the way back north: 1.7 miles round trip and 690 feet up. Leashed dogs are fine here.
  7. 3:45 p.m.Head downSunset in Washington is 4:50. Deer, bears and turkeys cross Skyline Drive without warning.

Before you go

  • The lodge is the only mealThe Big Meadows Wayside stopped serving food Nov. 8. Its store is open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and its gas pumps, the only ones in the park, run around the clock by card.
  • Check the roadSkyline Drive closes in bad weather. Text SHENALERTS to 888777 for its status, or call 540-999-3500.
  • Dress for itThe Park Service says the mountain usually runs 10 degrees cooler than the valley. Cell coverage is unreliable.

The Spirit play for the title, and the party is free

The NWSL Championship is at Audi Field on Saturday at 8 p.m., and the home team is in it: the Washington Spirit against the Kansas City Current. Built out of cans

Editor's
pick

For one afternoon, teams from D.C. architecture and design firms take over the Great Hall of the National Building Museum and build sculptures out of canned food. This year's theme is historic U.S. monuments and landmarks. You watch them go up from noon to 4, it's free, and afterward the food goes to the Capital Area Food Bank. Bring a can. It's your ballot.

  1. 12:15 p.m.Yellow Line to Gallery PlaceFive stops from Crystal City, then two blocks east to the museum at 401 F St. NW.
  2. 12:30 p.m.The Great Hall, round oneThe teams start at noon. Until 1 there are drop-in activities, so you can find out how hard it is to stack real cans.
  3. 1:15 p.m.Lunch at Bantam King501 G St. NW, open 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. It's a one-minute walk from the museum. Ramen runs $17-$19, and a fried chicken plate is $16.25.
  4. 2:30 p.m.Round twoGo back and see how far the sculptures got. Building stops at 4.
  5. 3:15 p.m.The holiday market, on the way backF Street NW between Seventh and Ninth, between the museum and the Metro. It's open until 8 on Sundays.
  6. 4 p.m.Yellow Line homeFive stops back.

Before you go

  • Bring a canA canned good gets you a vote for your favorite sculpture.
  • Can't make Sunday?The finished sculptures stay up through Nov. 29 and come down Nov. 30. The museum is open Thursday through Monday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Thanksgiving Day. That leaves Monday, Nov. 23, and Friday through Sunday, Nov. 27-29.
  • Game nightThe Wizards play Orlando at Capital One Arena at 7 p.m., so Gallery Place fills up toward evening.

The bagel ruling

This is a newsletter with a Yiddish name, so the bagel recommendations had better be right. New Jersey has the best bagels in the world. Take it from a Jew from Jersey. But I've done the field research around here, and one spot takes the cake.

  • The winner: Brooklyn Bagel Bakery

    2055 Wilson Blvd., Courthouse · Sat.-Sun. 7 a.m.-4 p.m., Mon.-Fri. 6:30 a.m.-3 p.m.

    The most consistent bagel around and the closest you'll get to a Jersey bagel: fresh and warm, a nice crisp shell, chewy inside. No Taylor ham, and the schmears aren't the best. I'm loyal anyway.

  • Bethesda Bagels, Rosslyn

    Local chain 1851 N. Moore St. · daily 7 a.m.-2 p.m.

    They've got the bagel down. Huge, with a crispy shell, and great spreads. They even have Taylor ham. They think it's called pork roll, but nobody's perfect.

  • Chewish Deli, Old Town

    807 Pendleton St., Alexandria · Wed.-Fri. 7 a.m.-2 p.m., Sat.-Sun. 8 a.m.-2 p.m.

    My spot in Old Town. Does everything well. Great spreads, and creative sandwiches like the pastrami and corned beef bagels: the Pastrami, Egg + Swiss and the Sunrise Reuben are $9.95 each.

It's close among all three. You can't go wrong.

Close to home

  • Duke's Burgers

    Water Park, 1621 Crystal Drive · Fri.-Sun. 11 a.m.-8:30 p.m.

    A burger counter at Water Park. It doesn't post prices online.
