The schlep meter

How far will you schlep?

Every pick gets a meter, so you know the trip before you commit.

No schlep Walk it From the Crystal City Metro on foot Long Bridge Park, Crystal Drive, the Water Park. Shoes on, out the door. Usually free
Short schlep One ride One Metro ride or a short drive Old Town, Potomac Yard, Del Ray, Shirlington. Home in time for anything. A fare or a few miles
Real schlep Into D.C. Over the river or with a transfer The Mall, Navy Yard, the Wharf. Worth planning around the Metro. Check the weekend track work
Big schlep Car only Far, and worth the gas Dark-sky parks, ginkgo groves, eagle marshes. A day trip with a reason. Gas, parking, snacks
